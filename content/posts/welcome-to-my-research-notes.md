This site is a home for work that benefits from more space than a paper abstract and less ceremony than a manuscript. I plan to use these notes to develop ideas in public, document useful research patterns, and connect technical results to broader questions about how intelligent systems reason.

## What will appear here

Most posts will fall into one of three categories:

- **Research notes** on language model reasoning, inference-time search, and reliable generation.
- **Paper reflections** that focus on the design choices, failed paths, and open questions that do not fit neatly into a publication.
- **Working methods** for experiments, reading, writing, and keeping research projects maintainable.

The goal is not to publish on a schedule. It is to make useful ideas easier to revisit, test, and discuss.

## A note on status

Unless a post says otherwise, these are working views rather than settled conclusions. I expect the best notes to change as new evidence arrives.
